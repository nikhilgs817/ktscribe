# What's new

## 2026-11-26 · Desktop 2.0 and phone 0.6.0

### Pocket Scribe Desktop

- **One page, new look.** A single page in the CarPlay Sync style: soft raised panels, a six-step rail on every session card, status badges, light and dark mode and gentle animations. **i** explains every feature; **Settings** has every control in one place.
- **vLLM and Claude.** Besides Ollama, connect any OpenAI-compatible server such as vLLM, or Claude in the cloud. Keys are encrypted with Windows and never shown again. Models are picked for you when you switch.
- **Meeting invite.** A follow-up invite drafted from the open questions and action items, with an agenda and prep. Copy it, or download .ics or .txt.
- **Claude bundle.** One zip with a brief, the transcript, the KT document, the summary and the screenshots, plus a goal you type, ready to give Claude.
- **Hands-free from the phone.** New phone recordings start processing as soon as Google Drive syncs them, and the finished KT document, invite and bundle are copied back to **Pocket Scribe › Outputs** in Drive.
- **KT document** replaces "runbook" everywhere, with **Write again** and **Previous version**.
- **Screenshots** have Fewer / Normal / More, **Find again** and **Describe all**.
- **Options** for the next recording: title, language, screenshot amount and words to know.
- Fixed: calendar invites now escape semicolons correctly.

### Pocket Scribe for Android

- Light and dark theme matching the desktop app, following the phone or set in **Settings › Appearance**.
- A status badge (**READY**, **● REC**, **WORKING**, **YOUR TURN**, **SYNCING**, **CHECK**), an **i** sheet on how it works, and a **Recordings** button on the main screen.
- Settings grouped into cards: Google Drive, Recordings, Appearance, Recording tips and Help.
- Recordings show **ON PHONE**, **SYNCING** or **ON DRIVE**.
- The Drive upload protocol is unchanged, so existing installs keep working.
